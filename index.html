Dodge the core. Destroy it to clear a level — or survive forever in Endless.
Levels
Destroy the core to clear all 20 levels — no time limit, just wear it down.
  • Sever shots to damage the core
  • Unlock new abilities (Q, F, R)
  • Star ratings for fast clears
Endless
No core, no time limit — just survive as long as you possibly can.
  • Difficulty ramps up forever
  • Score-boosting upgrades
  • Chase a new high score
HOW TO PLAY
WASD
Move around the arena (arrow keys also work)
SPACE
Jump — hop over ground shots, but locks your direction until you land. A committed dodge, not free movement.
SHIFT
Dash — brief burst of speed with full invulnerability. Can't be used mid-jump.
E
Press to slow every projectile in the arena — it runs until the energy drains out, or press again to cancel early. Energy regenerates on its own over time.
SEVER
Levels only — every shot trails a tail behind it; cutting through that tail destroys the shot and chips the core's health.
Levels: beat the core's health to 0 to win — there's no time limit, just survive long enough to wear it down. Endless: no core to destroy, just survive as long as you can for score. Both modes offer upgrade picks along the way, but upgrades reset at the start of every new level or run. Some upgrades unlock a whole new ability key — Q, F, and R are all locked until you pick them up.
