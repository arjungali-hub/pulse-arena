No core — just dodge. Both players face the same barrage; the last one still alive wins. One hit and you're out.

Co-op · Local 2-Player
Two players, one keyboard, same team. Pick a mode.
Survival
No core — just survive the barrage together, as long as you can. Die and you respawn after a few seconds, but if your partner falls while you're still down, you both lose.
Takedown
One shared, reinforced core. Sever shots together to tear it down. Same respawn rule — fall at the same time and the core wins.
